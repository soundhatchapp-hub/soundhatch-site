3. Identification is a best guess

SoundHatch uses an automatic sound-recognition model. It can be wrong, and it can miss birds that are singing. Don't rely on it for scientific surveys, safety decisions or anything important. For serious birding, use dedicated tools such as Merlin or eBird and local experts.

4. The Bird Oath, and being a good neighbour

  • Respect wildlife: keep your distance, don't disturb nests, and don't play recorded songs to attract birds.
  • Respect people: don't use SoundHatch to record others without their knowledge. Pay attention to your surroundings, traffic and private property while listening.
  • Follow local rules in parks and nature reserves.
