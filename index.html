Listen. Tap. Hatch.

No birding experience needed. Your phone helps you find what is out there; your own ears make it count.

1

Carry an egg

You get one egg at a time, picked for a bird that is actually singing near you this season.

2

Hear it, tap "I heard it!"

When you hear the song, tap. SoundHatch checks the last few seconds of sound and tells you if you got it.

3

Watch it hatch

Each confirmed bird hatches into your aviary, where it perches, preens and sings. Collect all of your city's regulars.

An aviary that looks like home

Your aviary grows in a landscape from your part of the world.

Quiet by design

An app that listens should earn your trust. So SoundHatch keeps as little as possible.

Sound leaves only when you tap

Listening happens on your phone. Only when you tap "I heard it!" or Collect are the last few seconds sent to be identified, then thrown away.

Your aviary lives on your phone

Progress is stored on your device. There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of your data. Ever.

Made in Amsterdam, by one person

An independent project. Identification runs on a small server in the EU.

The Bird Oath

Before your first egg, you make two promises.

"I will only record birds I truly hear."

"I will watch from a distance and never disturb them."

No leaderboards, no playback lures. Just you, paying attention.
