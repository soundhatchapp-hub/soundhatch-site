Help SoundHatch hatch

Google Play only lets a new app go public after at least 12 people have tested it for 14 days in a row. You'd be one of them.

Three steps, two minutes

  1. Accept the invitation to the "SoundHatch testers" Google Group. It arrives by email from Google Groups at the Gmail address you use on your Android phone. Didn't get one? Email the address below with that Gmail address and I'll add you.
  2. Opt in on this page, then tap the Google Play link it shows:
    play.google.com/apps/testing/net.soundhatch.app
  3. Install SoundHatch and keep it installed for 14 days. Opening it a few times while you're outside is plenty.

What I'd love from you

  • Try it on a walk, in a park, or by an open window in the morning.
  • Tell me one thing that confused you and one thing you liked. Use the Play Store's private feedback, or email soundhatch.app@gmail.com.
  • Please don't leave the test group early: testers who leave before 14 days don't count.

Privacy

Same as the public app: no account, no ads, no tracking, and sound only leaves your phone when you tap "I heard it!" or Collect. Your email address is visible only to me in the Google Group and Play Console. Read the privacy policy.
