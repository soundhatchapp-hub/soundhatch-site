Common questions

How do I hatch my egg?

Open Listen, tap Start Listening, and when you hear the bird your egg belongs to, tap I heard it!. SoundHatch checks the last few seconds of sound. Each confirmed hearing moves the egg closer to hatching. When a new egg moves into your nest, its card plays the song so you know what to listen for. Hear a bird you don't have yet? Tap Collect while it sings: once SoundHatch is sure which bird it is, its egg joins your queue (Listen → Eggs).

It keeps saying "Not quite". What can I do?

Get a little closer (without disturbing the bird), point the bottom of your phone towards the song, and tap while it is singing or right after. Wind, traffic and people talking make it harder. Dawn and early morning in spring are the best times by far. In autumn and winter many birds sing less, so give it time.

The first identification is slow.

If nobody has used the server for a while it needs a few seconds to wake up. The next tries are fast.

The app can't use my microphone or location.

Android: Settings → Apps → SoundHatch → Permissions. iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone (or Location Services) → SoundHatch. Location is optional; the microphone is needed to listen.

Does SoundHatch record me?

No. Listening happens on your phone in a short rolling buffer that is constantly overwritten. Only when you tap "I heard it!" or Collect are the last ~8 seconds sent for identification; the clip is analysed in memory and deleted immediately. Details in the privacy policy.

Which birds are in the game?

18 common, loud city birds: 10 for Europe and 8 for North America (House Sparrow and European Starling appear in both). More regions are planned.

I lost my aviary / got a new phone.

Progress is stored only on your device, with no account, so it can't be restored or moved at the moment. Cloud backup is on the wish list.

How do I delete my data?

Everything SoundHatch keeps about you is on your phone: use Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app. Our server doesn't store audio or location. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Free, with no ads and no tracking. SoundHatch Plus is an optional subscription (€0.99 a month or €7.99 a year) for the extras and a new drop each month; listening, hatching every bird, streaks and awards stay free.

Found a wrong identification?

Please email which bird you think it was, where (city is enough) and when. That helps tune the app for city conditions.
