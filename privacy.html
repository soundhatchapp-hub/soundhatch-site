Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version
  • The microphone listens on your phone. Sound only leaves your phone when you tap "I heard it!" or Collect.
  • That short clip (about 8 seconds) goes to our identification server in the EU (Amsterdam), is analysed in memory, and is deleted immediately. It is never saved or listened to by a person.
  • Your approximate location is sent with the clip so we only suggest birds that live in your region. It is not stored.
  • Your aviary, eggs and progress are stored only on your device.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell or share your data.

2. What SoundHatch does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses or phone numbers are collected in the app.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. We don't build profiles and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • No listening in the background. The microphone only runs while the Listen screen is open and you have started listening. It stops when you tap Stop or leave the app.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. What data is processed, and why

3.1 Microphone audio

While you are listening, the app keeps the last few seconds of sound in your phone's memory in a rolling buffer that is constantly overwritten. Nothing is recorded to storage and nothing is sent anywhere.

When you tap "I heard it!" or Collect (to collect the egg of a different bird), the app sends the last ~8 seconds of that buffer to our identification server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The server:

  • decodes and analyses the clip in memory with a bird-sound recognition model (Perch 2.0 by Google, running on our own server; the audio is not sent to Google);
  • returns the most likely species to your phone, usually within a second or two;
  • then discards the audio. It is never written to disk, never logged, never kept, and never used to train models.

If people are talking nearby when you tap, their voices may be part of the clip. It is handled in exactly the same way and discarded straight away. Nobody listens to clips.

Legal basis: performance of the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

3.2 Approximate location

When you start listening, the app asks your phone for an approximate position (Android "approximate location"; on iOS you can choose "Precise: Off"). It does not use background location.

When you tap "I heard it!" or Collect, the app sends coordinates rounded to roughly 10 km along with the clip. The server uses them only to work out your broad region (for example Europe or North America) so it doesn't suggest a bird that doesn't live there. The server does not store or log the coordinates.

On your phone, each bird you confirm is saved with the time and the approximate location of the detection, to power badges such as "heard in 3 different places". This stays on your device.

You can refuse location access. The app still works; identification is just less well filtered.

Legal basis: performance of the service (Art. 6(1)(b)).

3.3 Technical server logs

Like any internet service, our server necessarily receives your device's IP address when your phone connects. We use it only to deliver the response and to limit abuse (a cap on requests per minute). We do not store your IP address. Our server keeps a short technical log line per identification request with: the time, response status and duration, the size and length of the audio, the species returned, the broad region, and a salted, one-way hash of the IP address so that abusive traffic can be spotted without keeping the address itself. These logs are kept by our hosting provider for 7 days and then deleted automatically.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.4 Bird song playback

The bird songs you can play in the app are bundled with the app. Playing them makes no network request.

3.5 Game progress

Your eggs, aviary, field guide, streaks, badges, feathers and settings are stored only on your device (app storage). We cannot see them and cannot restore them for you. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, deletes them permanently.

3.6 Purchases

SoundHatch Plus is an optional subscription made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes the payment and the renewals under its own privacy policy. We receive no name, payment details or purchase history, and we run no server for purchases. The app only records on your phone that the subscription is active (and until when) and which months you held it, so it can unlock the features and the monthly drops and restore them on a new device through your store account.

3.7 Reminders

If you switch on reminders in Settings, the app schedules at most two notifications a day (the dawn chorus, and an evening nudge when a streak is at risk) on your phone itself. They are local notifications: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no push service and no device token. Turn them off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

3.8 "Help train SoundHatch" (optional, not active)

The app does not upload any recordings for training. In the future we may offer an optional setting that lets you donate confirmed clips to improve identification in city conditions. If we do, it will be off by default, we will update this policy first, explain exactly what is kept and for how long, and only upload clips if you switch it on (legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a), which you can withdraw at any time).

3.9 When you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and delete the conversation within 12 months after it is closed, unless we need to keep it longer to resolve a dispute. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)).

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because SoundHatch has no accounts and does not store audio or location on the server, we usually hold no data we can link to you. Your game data is on your phone and fully under your control.

To exercise a right, email soundhatch.app@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; elsewhere in the EU, the authority in your country.

California and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

7. Children

SoundHatch is made for adults and teenagers who enjoy the outdoors. It is not directed at children under 13, and in the EU we do not knowingly process personal data of children under 16 (the age of digital consent in the Netherlands) beyond what is described above, which is not stored. There are no accounts, chat or social features. The optional subscription goes through the phone's store account, with the store's own parental controls. If you believe a child has sent us personal data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS). The server stores no audio, no location and no user profiles, so there is very little to leak.

9. Changes

If we change how SoundHatch handles data, for example by adding crash reporting, a purchase server or the optional training-clip feature, we will update this page before the app version that makes the change, and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the store's "What's new" notes.
